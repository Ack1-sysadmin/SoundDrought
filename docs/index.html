Everything a browser tab can't do

It's the real soundcloud.com inside Windows' own Edge engine, so your login, likes, playlists and Go+ just work.

⏯

Media keys

Play, pause and skip from your keyboard, headset or the Windows media overlay.

◱

Mini player

Press Ctrl+M for a small always-on-top player with artwork, likes and a seek bar.

☀

Lives in the tray

Close the window and the music keeps playing. Click the icon to bring it back, middle-click to pause.

🎧

Discord status

Shows what you're listening to on your Discord profile, with a button to the track. You can turn it off.

⏭

Taskbar controls

Previous, play/pause and next right in the taskbar thumbnail.

⚡

Slim

About 1.5 MB, no bundled browser. It uses the WebView2 engine that ships with Windows.

FAQ

Short answers to the obvious questions.

Is this an official SoundCloud app?

No. SoundDrought is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with SoundCloud. It shows the regular soundcloud.com website and adds desktop features around it.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 and the free .NET 10 Desktop Runtime. WebView2 is already part of Windows.

Can I install it with winget?

Once the package is approved: winget install Ack1.SoundDrought

Is my login safe?

You log in on soundcloud.com itself, just like in your browser. Your session is stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SoundDrought and never leaves your PC.

Why the name?

Sound, minus the cloud. A drought. ☀️
